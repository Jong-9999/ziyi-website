About

Finance meets
First Principles

Cao Ziyi is a finance and economics professional with a deep focus on risk management, capital markets, and quantitative analysis. Currently serving as Head of FICC at Apeiron Holdings — a multi-strategy hedge fund in Singapore — and interning at the Agricultural Bank of China, he brings hands-on experience across the full spectrum of financial markets.

His academic foundation is equally formidable: a NUS Global Merit Scholar pursuing a rare double degree in Business (Finance) and Economics at the National University of Singapore, with a specialisation in Quantitative Economics. His analytical rigour was honed during a 9-month internship at the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), where he worked in the Capital and Liquidity Policy division, building regulatory dashboards and enhancing MAS's financial risk analysis capabilities to support banking system resilience.

Beyond finance, Cao holds a second major in Performing Arts (Music) from the Yong Siew Toh Conservatory of Music, serving as First Desk Double Bass in the NUS Symphony Orchestra — a testament to the discipline and precision he brings to everything he pursues.
